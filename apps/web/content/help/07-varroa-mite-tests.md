# Varroa Mite Tests

The **Varroa** tab on a hive records mite counts and compares each result with a seasonal action threshold from the **Honey Bee Health Coalition (HBHC)**.

## Add a mite test

1. Open the hive and choose the **Varroa** tab.
2. Tap **+ Add Mite Test**.
3. Enter the **Test Date**, **# of Bees** sampled (default 300) and **Mites Found**.
4. Add optional **Notes** (for example the test method).
5. Tap **Save Mite Test**.

As you type, the form shows the **Mite Load** (mites ÷ bees × 100, as a percentage), the **HBHC Threshold** for the test date's month, and a status. Bees sampled must be greater than 0 and mites cannot be negative.

## Seasonal thresholds used by the app

| Test date month | Threshold |
|---|---|
| January – March | 1% |
| April – August | 3% |
| September – October | 2% |
| November – December | 1% |

## Status labels

- **✅ OK** — mite load is below the threshold
- **⚠️ Above** — at or above the threshold
- **🔴 Critical** — at or above 1.5 × the threshold

The threshold in effect on the test date is saved with each test, so older results keep the threshold they were judged against.

## History and chart

- The history shows the **last 6 tests** in a table: Date, Mites/Bees, %, Limit and Status. Tap a row for details; the owner can **Edit** or **Delete**.
- **Mite Load by Season** is a chart of six seasonal periods ending with the current one: Jan–Feb, Mar, Apr–May, Jun–Aug, Sep–Oct, Nov–Dec. For each period it shows the latest result as a dot (green OK, amber above, red critical), a shaded bar for the range when there is more than one test, and a dashed red line for that period's threshold.
- Purple 👑 markers show **Queen Replacement** interventions in those periods.

Only the apiary owner can add, edit or delete mite tests.

The app records and compares results; what to do about a high count (treatment choice and timing) is a beekeeping decision outside this app's documentation.
