# Sharing Apiaries and Mentors

You can give a **mentor** read-only access to an apiary so they can review your hive records.

## Share an apiary (owner)

1. On the apiary list screen, tap **⚙️ Manage Apiaries**.
2. Tap **Share** beside the apiary.
3. Search the list of mentors who are accepting students (by name, location or email) and select one.
4. Tap **Confirm Access**.

The mentor then has **read-only** access to all hives in that apiary. They cannot edit or delete anything. If you try to share with the same mentor twice, the app tells you it is already shared.

## View an apiary shared with you

1. On the apiary list screen, tick **Show Shared**.
2. Shared apiaries appear in the dropdown under **Shared with Me** as "Apiary name (from Owner name)".
3. Open it as normal to see its hives, inspections, interventions, mite tests and tasks.

## Who can change what

- **Only the apiary owner can make changes.** If a viewer tries to edit, add or delete, the app shows "Only the apiary owner can make changes."
- Viewers see the same screens, but the edit controls do nothing for them.
- Your own apiaries are always fully editable by you.

Mentors are set up by the BeekTools administrators; an administrator has extra **Admin** pages for managing mentors, users, feature requests and beta sign-ups. Regular users do not see these.
