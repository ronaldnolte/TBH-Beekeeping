# Tasks

Tasks are your to-do list for beekeeping jobs.

## Two places to create tasks

- **Dashboard (apiary list screen):** **My Upcoming Tasks** → **+ New Task**. These are general tasks and show "General Task" as their location.
- **A hive's Tasks tab:** open the hive, choose **Tasks**, tap **+ Add**. These belong to that hive and show the apiary and hive name.

## Task form

- **Task Title** (required)
- **Priority:** Low, Medium (default) or High
- **Due Date** (defaults to today; can be cleared)
- **Description** (optional)

Tap **Add Task** (or **Update Task** when editing). Tasks are assigned to the person who creates them.

## Working with the list

- Columns: Done, Pri, Due, Title, Description, Location.
- Tick the **Done** checkbox to complete a task; untick to reopen it.
- Completed tasks are hidden unless you tick **Show Completed**.
- Overdue, uncompleted tasks show their due date in red.
- Order: open tasks before completed ones, then earliest due date, then higher priority, then newest.
- **✎** edits a task, **×** deletes it (after confirmation). Tap a row to see **Task Details**.

The dashboard list **My Upcoming Tasks** shows every task assigned to you across all your hives and apiaries. A hive's Tasks tab shows only that hive's tasks.

Only the apiary owner can add, edit or delete tasks on a hive.
