# Ask AI

**Ask AI** is a built-in beekeeping assistant powered by Google Gemini.

## Using it

1. Open an apiary.
2. Tap **🤖 Ask AI** in the toolbar.
3. Type your question (for example "Is it too cold to inspect today?") and tap **Ask Question** (or press Enter; Shift+Enter makes a new line).
4. The answer appears in the panel. You can ask another question afterwards; each question is answered on its own.

You must be logged in to use it.

## What it knows

The assistant is given:

- The **location** of the apiary you opened it from, and an **estimated season** worked out from today's date and the apiary's hemisphere.
- The **hive types** in that apiary (so it can tailor advice, or ask which hive you mean if you have incompatible types, such as Top Bar and Langstroth).

It does **not** see your inspections, mite tests, tasks or weather forecast. If your question depends on them, include the details in your question.

## What it does

- Answers beekeeping questions concisely (aims for under about 200 words) with simple formatting.
- Politely declines questions that are not about bees, hives or apiary management.
- It can make mistakes — check anything important.

## Messages you might see

- **You must be logged in to ask questions.** — sign in first.
- **The hive is busy (Rate Limit Reached). Please try again in a minute.** — too many requests; wait and retry.
- **AI Service is currently unavailable** or **Failed to process request** — a temporary problem; try again later or use the feedback button.

## Privacy

Your question and apiary context are sent to Google Gemini to produce the answer. Saving questions and answers to the BeekTools history is controlled by an administrator setting and is off unless enabled. Deleting your account clears any AI history (see `13-account-privacy-support.md`).

To get help using BeekTools itself (not beekeeping advice), read the other documents in this library or use the feedback button.
