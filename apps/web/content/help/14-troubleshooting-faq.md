# Troubleshooting and FAQ

**Why can't I delete my apiary?**
An apiary can only be deleted when it has no hives. Delete the hives (**✏️ Edit** on the apiary dashboard, then **🗑️ Delete**) or **Move** them to another apiary first.

**I can't change my hive's type.**
The hive model is locked after creation. Create a new hive of the type you want. You can still rename the hive, edit notes, and change the number of bars.

**"Only the apiary owner can make changes."**
You are viewing an apiary that someone shared with you. Shared access is read-only.

**The forecast doesn't load, or shows "Invalid Postal Code".**
Check the apiary's location (**⚙️ Manage Apiaries → Edit**). Make sure the country matches the postal code. If the postal code isn't recognised, switch to the **Coordinates** tab and enter latitude and longitude. A placeholder postal code of 00000 has no weather.

**There's no weather chip on the apiary list.**
The chip only appears on wide screens, after you select an apiary that has a valid location.

**The forecast says 7 days, but I read 14 somewhere.**
The forecast covers the next 7 days.

**How do I undo a deleted hive, inspection or snapshot?**
You can't — deletions are permanent. The app asks you to confirm first.

**Does BeekTools work without internet?**
It saves directly to the cloud, so you need a connection to load or save records.

**What hive types can I use?**
Langstroth (8- and 10-frame), UK National, Dadant, Warré, Top Bar (Kenyan), Layens, Long Langstroth and Dartington. See `03-hives.md`.

**How often should I inspect?**
That is a beekeeping question rather than an app one. A commonly used guide is every 7–10 days in the active season, less in autumn and winter; use the forecast to pick a good hour.

**How do I stop my bars changing when I tap them?**
Tapping a bar cycles its status and saves instantly. To look without editing, open a snapshot from **History** — that view is read-only.

**Where is my data stored? Can others see it?**
In your account in the cloud, separate from other users. Others see it only if you share an apiary with them.

**Where do I report a bug or ask for a feature?**
The envelope button in the header, or **Ideas** for the public roadmap (`12-feedback-and-roadmap.md`).
