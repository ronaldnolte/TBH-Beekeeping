# Getting Started

## Accounts and guest mode

- Sign up with an email address and password, or choose **Continue as Guest** on the login page to look around with demo data.
- The guest account is shared and meant for testing. When a guest logs out, the guest data is wiped and a small demo apiary is restored.
- Guests cannot submit feature requests (see `12-feedback-and-roadmap.md`).
- Forgot your password? Use the password reset page (**Reset Password**), enter your email, and follow the emailed link to **Set New Password**.

## First steps

1. Create an apiary — a name plus a location (postal code or coordinates). See `02-apiaries.md`.
2. Add a hive to it — pick the hive model and, for bar hives, the number of bars. See `03-hives.md`.
3. Set up the hive's configuration and capture a **snapshot**. See `04-bar-and-box-configuration.md`.
4. Record your first **inspection** (`05-inspections.md`).
5. Check the **forecast** for your apiary to choose a good inspection time (`09-weather-forecast.md`).

If you have no apiaries yet, the apiary list shows **+ Create First Apiary**. After you create your first apiary the app opens it for you.

## Internet connection

BeekTools saves your records directly to the cloud, so you need an internet connection to load and save data. Your data is then available from any device you sign in on.

## Install it like an app (PWA)

BeekTools runs in a browser and can be added to your home screen.

- **Android (Chrome):** open the site, tap the ⋮ menu, then **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**. An **Install** button may also appear in the app's header.
- **iPhone/iPad:** open the site in **Safari** (not Chrome), tap the Share button, then **Add to Home Screen**.

## Native Android app

A native Android app is in closed beta on Google Play. To join, open the beta page on the BeekTools site (**Join Android Beta**), enter the email address of your Google account, and follow the Google Play testing link.

## Other BeekTools tools

- **Hive Forecast** (forecast.beektools.com) is a standalone forecast app: enter any postal code, no account needed. A link to it is at the bottom of the forecast screen.
