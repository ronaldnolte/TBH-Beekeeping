# Feedback and Feature Roadmap

## Send a private message

The amber **envelope button** in the page header opens **Send Feedback**:

1. Type your message (suggestions, bugs or questions).
2. Optionally add your email if you would like a reply.
3. Tap **Send Feedback**.

If sending fails, the dialog shows an error and suggests emailing feedback@beektools.com.

## Feature roadmap (public ideas and votes)

Open it with the **Ideas** button on the apiary list screen, or **View Roadmap & Vote →** in the feedback dialog. The page is titled **Feedback & Roadmap**.

- **Community Requests** are sorted by votes, then newest.
- Tap the **▲** box on a request to vote; tap again to remove your vote. You must be logged in to vote.
- Each request has a status: **pending**, **planned** or **completed**.
- **+ Submit Idea**: give a **Title** (up to 100 characters) and a **Description**, then **Submit Request**. New ideas start as pending.
- Guest users cannot submit ideas ("Login Required") — sign up for a free account to contribute.
