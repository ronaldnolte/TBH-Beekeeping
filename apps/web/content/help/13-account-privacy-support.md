# Account, Privacy and Support

## Cost

BeekTools is free to use.

## Delete your account and data

Use the **Account & Data Deletion** page: it explains what is removed and gives the email address to write to. Send the request **from the email address linked to your account**. Deleting permanently removes your account credentials, all apiary and hive records, and all AI interaction history. It cannot be undone. Requests are typically processed within 48 hours.

## Exporting your data

There is no export feature yet. Your records stay available in your account in the cloud.

## Privacy summary

- Your data is used to run the app and show your records on your devices.
- It is not sold and is not used for advertising.
- Services used: **Supabase** (database and sign-in), **Google Analytics** (anonymous usage statistics), **Google Gemini** (the Ask AI feature) and **Expo/EAS** (building and delivering the mobile app).
- Each user's data is separate from other users'. Another person sees your apiary only if you share it with them (`10-sharing-and-mentors.md`), and then read-only.

The full text is on the **Privacy Policy** page.

## Getting help

- **Envelope button** in the header: send a message to the BeekTools team (`12-feedback-and-roadmap.md`).
- **Ideas / roadmap**: suggest and vote on features.
- Email feedback@beektools.com if the in-app message fails.
